Somewhere between design and development.

I'm a Computer Science and AI student who enjoys bringing ideas to life through technology and thoughtful design.

My interest in web development grew from a curiosity about how things work, and how they could work better. Along the way, I found myself drawn to the space between engineering and visual design: where a working product also has to feel intentional, usable and human.

I approach problems by breaking them into small, testable pieces. Whether it's a MERN-stack application, a blockchain risk engine, or a frontend interface, the goal is the same: build something useful, keep it simple, and iterate from there.
